About Standard Rope & Hardware

A Legacy of Trust Since 1970

Three generations of one Dewas family, dedicated to a single trade: supplying rope, net and hardware that businesses can rely on.

Raja Ram Patel Market · Dewas

Where It Began

Raja Ram Patel Market, Dewas

Aziz Ur Rahman Khan carries forward a business his family built counter by counter — from hand-laid cotton rope sold to local farmers, to becoming a recognised supplier for industries, transport companies and construction firms across Central India.

The address has stayed the same for over five decades. What's changed is the scale of who relies on it.

The Journey

Five Decades, One Address

1970

The first coil is sold

Aziz Ur Rahman Khan's father opens a small rope counter in Dewas' Raja Ram Patel Market, supplying farmers and cart operators with hand-laid cotton and jute rope.

1985

Industrial demand arrives

As Dewas' textile and engineering units grow, the shop expands into synthetic and nylon rope for transport binding and machine lashing.

1998

Net solutions added

Shade nets and agricultural netting are introduced to serve the region's expanding farming community through harsh summers.

2010

Regional supplier status

Standard Rope & Hardware becomes a recognised bulk supplier to construction and warehousing companies across Malwa.

Today

Three generations of trust

Now run with the same hands-on attention to quality, serving customers within a 500 km radius of Dewas.
